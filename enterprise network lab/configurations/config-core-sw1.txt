! Role      Layer 3 core. Holds every SVI except VLAN 60, performs all

!
!   The five access lists below are applied to their SVIs, but Packet Tracer
!   does not render `ip access-group` statements in running-config. They are
!   therefore absent from this capture on every Vlan interface.
!
!   Applied as:
!     Vlan10 → HR-IN in     Vlan20 → FIN-IN in    Vlan30 → SAL-IN in
!     Vlan50 → SERVERS-IN in, SERVERS-PROTECT out
!     Vlan99 → MGMT-PROTECT out
!
!   Enforcement was verified functionally rather than by inspection — see the
!   test results document, where eighteen access-control tests confirm every
!   permit and deny the policy specifies. `show access-lists` output for this
!   device is in the companion file.
!
! NOTE 2 — CREDENTIALS REMOVED
!

! This is the structural basis of Guest isolation (2.3.4).
!
